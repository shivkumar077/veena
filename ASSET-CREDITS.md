# Welcome landscape attribution

The welcome experience uses two art-directed, optimized WebP crops of the same
photograph:

- `public/assets/welcome/welcome-landscape.webp`
- `public/assets/welcome/welcome-portrait.webp`

**Original:** “20200409 Housaka, Cherry blossom trees in Housaka, Oguni,
Nagaoka, Niigata” by [Tyutyanya](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:Tyutyanya).

**Source:** [Wikimedia Commons file page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:20200409_Housaka,_Cherry_blossom_trees_in_Housaka,_Oguni,_Nagaoka,_Niigata.jpg).

**License:** [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
(CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The images were cropped for landscape and portrait layouts, resized, lightly
adjusted for color, contrast, and brightness, and converted from JPEG to WebP.
These adapted images are offered under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.

The photo depicts cherry trees beside the Housaka river with forested hills in
the distance. Individual branches are not separately animated.
